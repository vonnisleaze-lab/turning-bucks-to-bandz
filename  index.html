Privacy and data transparency

This preview does not have a server-side user database. Some tools save information to your own browser's local storage, which can remain on a shared device. Email inquiry buttons prepare a message in your email app; nothing is sent until you choose to send it. External links such as Instagram are subject to those services' own policies. Before collecting personal information on a published site, a reviewed privacy notice and appropriate safeguards are required.

Service, course and payment information

Course catalogs, paid membership tiers, resource purchases, and service packages are concepts for demonstration until officially launched. No guaranteed business earnings, financial outcomes, or accreditation are offered. Educational content is general information, not individualized legal, tax, investment, or financial advice.
